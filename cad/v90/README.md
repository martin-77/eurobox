# Eurobox carrier v90

V90 adds a full-length two-pin guide between the box clamp and the base while keeping the complete V80 clamp travel.

V90-native changed parts:

- `base_left` / `base_right`: two full-length cylindrical guide sleeves through the remote box-clamp crosshead.
- `clamp`: two integral Ø10 mm guide pins with lightly rounded tips.

Guide geometry:

- pin diameter: Ø10.00 mm
- base guide bore: Ø10.15 mm
- two pin axes remain fully engaged through the complete base crosshead at every clamp setting
- maximum design gap clamp→base: 30 mm
- pins may protrude about 30 mm behind the base at maximum gap
- guide pins are placed 25 mm inboard from the two existing clamp screw axes

Complete printable V90 set:

- base_left
- base_right
- bushing_half
- clamp
- knob
- lead_nut
- lead_nut_pin_clip
- lead_nut_retaining_pin
- lead_screw
- rack_hand_knob
- rack_lower
- rack_nut_retainer
- rack_pin
- rack_pin_clip

All components other than the two bases and clamp are promoted unchanged from V80 into the V90 namespace. Where V80 provides FCStd/STEP source files, they are copied as well. Printable-only V80 parts retain their SCAD source under `SCAD/`; the V90 clamp itself is generated by `scripts/build_v90_guided_clamp.py`.

Every V90 STL is hard-checked as canonical **binary STL** before publication. ASCII STL is rejected.
