# V70 retaining clips v3 — tighter physical-fit candidates

The v2 pin remains unchanged and stays the corrected 28.15 mm lead-nut retaining pin.

The v3 clips keep the original compact C-clip form but increase preload. They
are installed sideways directly onto the reduced groove and therefore do not
need to open over the larger shaft.

## Lead-nut retaining clip v3

- target groove: Ø2.40 mm
- relaxed ID: Ø2.10 mm
- diametral preload: 0.30 mm
- throat: 2.00 mm
- flared entry: 2.60 mm
- outer diameter: Ø6.40 mm
- thickness: 1.30 mm
- groove width: 1.60 mm -> 0.15 mm nominal axial clearance per side

## Rack-pin clip v3

- target groove: Ø3.10 mm
- relaxed ID: Ø2.80 mm
- diametral preload: 0.30 mm
- throat: 2.70 mm
- flared entry: 3.30 mm
- outer diameter: Ø6.40 mm
- thickness: 1.30 mm

Both STLs are binary STL files generated locally and validated watertight,
single-component before upload.
