# V70 retaining clips v4

Physical-fit candidates only. Do not promote to V80 until accepted.

Reference geometry:
`cad/v50/STL/eurobox_v50_plate_retainer_clip.stl`

V4 deliberately returns to the original V50 plate-retainer topology:
a nearly closed circular ring with one narrow rectangular side opening.
There is no flared/V-shaped lead-in.

## Lead-nut pin clip v4
- target groove: Ø2.40 mm
- relaxed clip ID: Ø2.10 mm
- diametral preload: 0.30 mm
- side opening: 1.60 mm
- outer diameter: Ø7.00 mm
- thickness: 1.30 mm

## Rack-pin clip v4
- target groove: Ø3.10 mm
- relaxed clip ID: Ø2.80 mm
- diametral preload: 0.30 mm
- side opening: 2.15 mm
- outer diameter: Ø7.50 mm
- thickness: 1.30 mm

The opening ratios are based on the proven V50 plate-retainer clip
(3.80 / 4.90 = 0.7755), rounded slightly tighter.

Both STLs are committed as true binary STL Git blobs, not ASCII STL.
